# Preliminary trademark collision check: OTTIE / OTTI

Date checked: 2026-08-22  
Scope: exact and selected similar word marks, with emphasis on software/SaaS/AI-related Nice classes 9 and 42 in the United States, WIPO Madrid System, and the European Union.  
Nature of work: preliminary naming screen only; not legal advice or a freedom-to-operate opinion.

## Bottom line

Neither **OTTIE** nor **OTTI** is a clean candidate for a coding-agent product.

- **OTTIE is the clearest blocker.** Tessares owns an active international registration for the exact standard-character mark in classes 9, 38, and 42. It is based on an EU registration and is also actively registered in the United States. Its class 9 goods explicitly include application software, computer programs, embedded software, and related computer hardware.
- **OTTI is also high-risk.** Yi, hong-jun owns an active Madrid registration for the exact standard-character mark in class 9. Its US extension is live and under examination. The US goods include downloadable software and downloadable digital images of cartoon otter characters, making it especially close to an otter-branded software project.
- The one-letter spelling difference does not create a safe separation. **OTTIE** and **OTTI** are visually and phonetically very close. That is a risk inference, not a legal conclusion.

Recommendation: **reject both names before package/project renaming** and generate a more distinctive coined name. Do not spend engineering effort migrating package names to either candidate.

Relevant coverage:

- WIPO class 9 wording includes downloadable cryptocurrency software, e-wallets, software for blockchain transactions, downloadable mobile emoticons/graphics and NFT-authenticated digital files.
- The current US identification is narrower/more explicit and includes downloadable software plus digital image files of memes and **cartoon otter characters** authenticated by NFTs.

Assessment: **high naming risk despite the pending US status.** It is an exact class 9 software mark, and the otter-character language overlaps the proposed software mascot identity. A pending application can still create opposition, examination, and priority complications.

## Legal and practical limits

- Trademark risk depends on jurisdiction, actual goods/services, channels of trade, mark similarity, priority, and use, not just exact-name hits or Nice class numbers.
- This screen does not cover every national register, unregistered/common-law rights, company names, domains, app stores, or local-language phonetic equivalents.
- A professional clearance opinion should include target markets, intended goods/services wording, owner/use investigation, and attorney review.
- Registry status can change. Re-check immediately before any public launch or filing.
