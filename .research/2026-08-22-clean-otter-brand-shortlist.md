# Clean otter brand shortlist

Date: 2026-08-22  
Decision priority: spoken appeal first, followed by project/package conflicts and preliminary trademark risk.  
Status: naming recommendation only; no repository rename has been performed.

## Decision

### Recommended: Lutrilo

- Pronunciation: **loo-TREE-lo** (`lu-TRI-lo`, three beats).
- Why it wins: the `ee` to `oh` movement and final `-lo` give it the most musical, complete sound among the candidates that survived collision screening. It feels friendly without sounding like a child's nickname or an IT utility.
- Brand story: inspired by *Lutra*, the zoological genus of Eurasian otters. It should not be described as a Latin word that literally means this product or assistant.
- Taiwan use: keep the official name in Latin letters. `露翠洛` is usable as an informal pronunciation aid, not a recommended legal or official Chinese name.
- Verified preliminary screen: no exact PyPI, npm, GitHub repository, major product/company, or mobile-app collision was found in the completed screen. Official USPTO live searches in Nice classes 9, 38, and 42 found no exact, `LUTRI*` prefix, or one-edit hit. Taiwan TIPO had no exact hit; nearby records make it slightly less clear than Lutrimi, but no decisive blocker was identified.

Suggested introduction:

> **Lutrilo** *(loo-TREE-lo)* — your little otter companion for building things.

The mascot must carry the immediate animal recognition: people unfamiliar with *Lutra* will not infer “otter” from the word alone.

### Backup: Lutrimi

- Pronunciation: **loo-TREE-mee**.
- It is the cleanest preliminary legal/namespace survivor and sounds warm.
- It ranks second because the repeated `ee` sound and `-mi` ending lean younger and more pet-like. In Taiwan, an intuitive `米` rendering also adds food/snack and cute-character associations.
- Choose it only if the intended personality is explicitly a soft personal companion.

## Eliminated after sound-first review

| Name | Sound assessment | Reason eliminated |
|---|---|---|
| Lutori | Best pure pronunciation (`loo-TOR-ee`) | Existing programming-related business; unacceptable category collision |
| Lutuno | Rounded and easy (`loo-TOO-no`) | Existing Lutuno commerce software/platform, live `lutuno.com`, exact GitHub repository, and a more crowded Taiwan software-mark neighborhood |
| Lutruna | Flowing but less stable | Frequently collapses toward Luna; feminine/fantasy/cosmetic associations dominate |
| Otrilo | Sleek | Active US class 9 near mark `OTIRILO`; Taiwan class 9/42 pending `Orito` |
| Lutrimi | Pleasant but cute-leaning | Retained only as the backup |

## What was checked

- Spoken form, likely first-read variants, hearing-to-spelling friction, Taiwan Mandarin approximations, and adult/cute balance.
- Exact package/project/product checks across the registries and sources recorded in the supporting reports.
- Official USPTO and Taiwan TIPO preliminary searches, emphasizing Nice classes 9, 38, and 42.
- Selected WIPO checks where the official portal returned reliable results.

## Limits before public adoption

- This is a preliminary naming screen, not legal advice or a trademark clearance opinion.
- WIPO exact searches for Lutrilo intermittently failed, and EUIPO/TMview could not be reliably automated. Do not claim worldwide availability.
- Registry and project-name availability is time-sensitive and does not reserve a name.
- Before a public launch or trademark filing, manually repeat WIPO/EUIPO/TMview and target-country common-law/company-name checks, then run a small human pronunciation test: show the word and ask people to say it; separately say it and ask people to spell it.
