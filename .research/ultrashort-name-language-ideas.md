# Ultra-short coined name ideas, round two

Date: 2026-08-22  
Scope: 15 new names, each 4–5 ASCII letters and 2–3 syllables. All first-round names and previously rejected names are excluded. Screening covers English and Taiwan Mandarin speaking ease, oral memory, adult/warm tone, and obvious negative meaning only. **No availability, registry, domain, project, or trademark claim is made.**

## Clear first choice

**Luvo** — pronounced **LOO-vo**.

- Four letters and two open syllables.
- English and Taiwan Mandarin speakers can reproduce it without a consonant cluster or inserted vowel.
- `l`, `v`, and rounded `u/o` give it a soft but adult sound.
- `Lu-` can carry an honest “inspired by *Lutra*” story, while the complete name remains abstract enough to grow beyond a mascot.
- It does not resemble a common English word or common Taiwan Mandarin personal name.

Primary oral caution: listeners may write `Louvo` after hearing it. The spelling should accompany the first spoken introduction.

## Spoken-language observations

### English

- `Luvo`, `Lurio`, and `Orivo` have the clearest stress patterns without resembling ordinary English words.
- Three-vowel names are pleasant aloud but less reliable when spelled from hearing; `Luaro`, `Luero`, and `Riavo` need the written brand visible early.
- Final `-o` keeps the names more adult-neutral than final `-i/-y`.
- None of the 15 contains an obvious English insult, bodily term, or strongly negative ordinary word.

### Taiwan Mandarin

- Alternating open syllables (`lu-vo`, `o-ri-vo`, `o-ru-vi`) are easier than Latin-script clusters such as `lutr-`.
- Official Chinese characters should not be chosen during this stage. A neutral Latin spelling avoids accidentally adding beauty, food, gender, regional, or status meanings.
- `Ruvio` deserves extra caution if localized: the easiest `Ru-` character is often `魯`, which can carry “blunt/clumsy” associations. This does not affect the Latin name when spoken.
- No obvious Taiwan Mandarin vulgar or insulting near-sound surfaced among the top six.

## Mature versus cute balance

- Most adult-neutral: **Luvo, Orivo, Eluvo**.
- Warmest companion tone: **Lurio, Oruvi, Orilo**.
- Most abstract/least animal-specific: **Eruvo, Uvio, Riavo**.
- Potentially too whimsical if visual design is also cute: **Rovu**.
