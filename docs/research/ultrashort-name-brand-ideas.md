# Ultra-short coined brand-name ideas

Date: 2026-08-22  
Scope: project display name, package name candidate, and lowercase CLI  
Status: phonetic ideation only; **no availability, registrability, or trademark claim**

## Brief

- Exactly 4-5 ASCII letters; no six-letter names.
- Two or three syllables in the recommended English pronunciation.
- Pronunciation first: warm, mature, and comfortable to repeat in conversation.
- The otter association may come from the mascot and visual system rather than the word itself.
- Excludes every first-round candidate and all previously rejected names, including Ottie, Otti, Lutori, Lutuno, and Otrilo.

## Ranked top 6

### 1. Arovi

- CLI: `arovi`
- Pronunciation: **uh-ROH-vee** /əˈroʊ.vi/
- Construction: *around* + *roving* + a flowing `-vi` cadence
- Brand sound: open, assured, and companion-like. It has motion without sounding like a mobility or infrastructure product.

### 2. Aluma

- CLI: `aluma`
- Pronunciation: **uh-LOO-muh** /əˈluː.mə/
- Construction: *aqua* + *lumen*
- Brand sound: calm light on water. The repeated open vowels feel warm while the middle `lum` gives it substance.

### 3. Miora

- CLI: `miora`
- Pronunciation: **mee-OR-uh** /miˈɔː.rə/
- Construction: *meander* + *aura*
- Brand sound: lyrical but adult; suitable for a named companion rather than a utility.

### 4. Evaro

- CLI: `evaro`
- Pronunciation: **eh-VAR-oh** /ɛˈvɑː.roʊ/
- Construction: *ever* + *roam*
- Brand sound: steady and capable, with a subtle “always alongside” story.

### 5. Melu

- CLI: `melu`
- Pronunciation: **MEH-loo** /ˈmɛ.luː/
- Construction: *mellow* compressed into a clean two-syllable form
- Brand sound: the softest candidate without becoming babyish; excellent for a reassuring mascot voice.

### 6. Oleni

- CLI: `oleni`
- Pronunciation: **oh-LEH-nee** /oʊˈlɛ.ni/
- Construction: *open* + *lean in*
- Brand sound: personable and attentive; reads naturally as a companion's name.

## Sound comparison

| Name | Spoken strength | Emotional register | Mascot relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arovi | Clear stress and easy consonants | Curious, capable | A companion who explores alongside you |
| Aluma | Strong vowel harmony | Calm, luminous | An otter floating in reflected light |
| Miora | Memorable three-beat cadence | Thoughtful, graceful | A character with a gentle proper name |
| Evaro | Firm center and rounded close | Dependable, mature | A steady roaming partner |
| Melu | Effortless two-syllable call | Reassuring, warm | Best for a soft-spoken mascot voice |
| Oleni | Naturally name-like | Attentive, personable | Best for treating the mascot as a character |

## Recommendation

For a brand led by spoken usability, advance **Arovi**, **Aluma**, and **Melu** first:

- **Arovi** has the strongest balance of warmth, movement, and maturity.
- **Aluma** creates the richest visual world for a water mascot.
- **Melu** is the easiest to turn into a reassuring character voice.

This ranking deliberately ignores availability. Before presenting any as adoptable, run separate exact and phonetic checks across packages, repositories, products, companies, domains, common CLI commands, and relevant trademark databases.
