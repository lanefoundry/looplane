# Preliminary trademark screen: RIVUMI / NUVIMI

Date checked: 2026-08-22  
Scope: exact, prefix, and one-edit screening focused on Nice classes 9, 38, and 42.  
Nature of work: preliminary naming screen only; not legal advice or a freedom-to-operate opinion.

## Decision and ranking

1. **RIVUMI — PASS; preferred candidate**
2. **NUVIMI — PASS on the narrow query, but not recommended without deeper review**

Both exact names returned zero in the checked USPTO and WIPO Madrid searches. `RIVUMI` ranks clearly higher because the broader `RIVU-` US software/technology space contains only a few more distant marks. The `NUVI-` space is crowded with 35 live US records in classes 9/38/42, including Garmin's active `NÜVI` registration and active `NUVIU` software/SaaS registrations.

|---|---:|

Assessment: **PASS**. Of the two candidates, this occupies the less crowded naming space and is the recommended survivor.
