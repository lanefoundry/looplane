# Short brand-name ideas for an otter companion

Date: 2026-08-22  
Scope: project name, package display name, and lowercase CLI candidate  
Status: phonetic ideation only; **no package, project, domain, company, or trademark availability claim**

## Constraints

- 4-6 ASCII letters
- 2-3 syllables in the suggested English pronunciation
- Warm and mature; friendly without sounding like a children's product
- Able to pair naturally with an otter mascot
- No `patch`, `code`, `dev`, `agent`, or `AI`
- Excludes Ottie, Otti, Lutori, Lutuno, and Otrilo

## Top 8 by brand sound

### 1. Lumo

- CLI: `lumo`
- Pronunciation: **LOO-moh** /ˈluː.moʊ/
- Construction: *luminous* + *motion*
- Why it works: round vowels make it warm and effortless to say; light on moving water gives the mascot a visual world without making the product sound technical.

### 2. Elori

- CLI: `elori`
- Pronunciation: **eh-LOR-ee** /ɛˈlɔː.ri/
- Construction: *eddy* + *lore* + a flowing `-i` ending
- Why it works: feels like a capable companion's proper name; distinctive rhythm, mature tone, and enough softness for an otter character.

### 3. Rillo

- CLI: `rillo`
- Pronunciation: **RIL-oh** /ˈrɪl.oʊ/
- Construction: *rill* (a small stream) + rounded `-o`
- Why it works: short, highly pronounceable, and unmistakably waterside without literally naming the animal.

### 4. Rivia

- CLI: `rivia`
- Pronunciation: **RIV-ee-uh** /ˈrɪv.i.ə/
- Construction: *river* + *via* (a way or journey)
- Why it works: suggests a companion moving alongside the user; fluid but not overly cute.

### 5. Marea

- CLI: `marea`
- Pronunciation: **muh-RAY-uh** /məˈreɪ.ə/
- Construction: *mare* (sea) + a tide-like open-vowel cadence
- Why it works: calm, expansive, and polished; supports sea-otter imagery particularly well.

### 6. Cova

- CLI: `cova`
- Pronunciation: **KOH-vuh** /ˈkoʊ.və/
- Construction: *cove* + open `-a`
- Why it works: conveys shelter and trust, useful for positioning the product as a safe companion rather than a tool.

### 7. Luria

- CLI: `luria`
- Pronunciation: **LOOR-ee-uh** /ˈlʊr.i.ə/
- Construction: softened *Lutra* echo + *aria*
- Why it works: subtly carries the otter-family sound while remaining lyrical and adult.

### 8. Avelo

- CLI: `avelo`
- Pronunciation: **uh-VELL-oh** /əˈvɛl.oʊ/
- Construction: *aqua* + *velocity* + rounded `-o`
- Why it works: gentle forward movement; feels active without sounding like developer infrastructure.

## Quick comparison

| Name | Best quality | Main risk to test later |
|---|---|---|
| Lumo | Warmest and easiest to say | Short names often have extensive prior use |
| Elori | Strongest companion character | Water/otter meaning is not immediately visible |
| Rillo | Clearest water association | May be heard or spelled as Rilo |
| Rivia | Best journey metaphor | Could carry unrelated fictional/geographic associations |
| Marea | Most elegant sea-otter world | Pronunciation may vary by language |
| Cova | Strongest shelter/trust idea | Very short and likely to be crowded |
| Luria | Best subtle otter-family echo | Also reads naturally as a surname/name |
| Avelo | Best gentle-motion brand | Construction is invented and needs a brand story |

## Recommendation

Take **Lumo, Elori, Rillo, Rivia, and Marea** into the next phase, but do not choose among them from this note alone. This ordering is strictly about spoken brand quality.

The next gate should independently screen exact and phonetic collisions across:

1. PyPI, npm, GitHub, Homebrew, crates.io, app stores, and common CLI commands.
2. Search engines, software directories, company registries, domains, and major social handles.
3. WIPO, USPTO, EUIPO, and TIPO, especially Nice classes 9, 42, and adjacent 38.
4. A hear-and-spell test: say each name once to English speakers and ask them to type it.
5. A mascot test: show name-only, then name-plus-otter, to measure how much visual help each candidate requires.
