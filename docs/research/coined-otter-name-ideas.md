# Coined otter brand-name ideas

Date: 2026-08-22  
Scope: English-friendly project, Python distribution, and lowercase CLI name  
Status: ideation plus lightweight collision elimination; **not trademark clearance or legal advice**

## Brief

- Warm and companion-like, but not childish.
- 5-9 ASCII letters, 2-3 syllables, natural as a lowercase CLI command.
- Avoid `patch`, `code`, `dev`, `agent`, and `AI`.
- Prefer an intuitive otter, river, cove, floating, or companion association.
- Excluded names: PatchOtter, PatchRaft, Ottie, Otti, Paca, Capybara, Axolotl, Otter, Otterly.

The strongest naming root is **Lutra**: ITIS lists *Lutra* as the valid genus name for Eurasian otters. This makes a coined `lutr-` name more defensible as an otter reference than a generic cute-animal syllable, while avoiding the crowded literal word `otter`.

## Scoring

Each dimension is scored 1-10. Total is out of 40.

- **Pron.**: predictable English pronunciation and ease of saying aloud.
- **Memory**: distinctiveness and recall.
- **Otter**: strength of otter, water, or companion association.
- **Tone**: warm and characterful without sounding juvenile or like an enterprise IT product.

Scores are brand judgments, not factual availability claims.

## Shortlist

### 1. Lutrali

Best strategic construction: **Lutra + ally**. It encodes both the mascot and the product relationship without sounding like an IT utility. The spoken form should be standardized as “loo-TRAH-lee.” Its main drawback is that users who do not know *Lutra* will initially need the otter mark/mascot to complete the association.

### 2. Lutrilo

Best purely verbal brand: easy rhythm, natural lowercase command, and close enough to *Lutra* to support the otter story. It has less explicit “companion” meaning than Lutrali but may be easier to hear, spell, and remember.

### 3. Lutruna

Best character name: calm, warm, and suitable for an assistant that stays with the user. The `luna` association gives it emotional texture, but it is also more likely to overlap with human/fantasy names.

### 4. Lutrimi

Best soft/cute balance: compact and affectionate without using an obvious diminutive such as `-ie`. The construction is less self-explanatory, so its success depends on visual identity and voice.

### 5. Lutrimo

Best mature alternative to Lutrimi. It sounds a little more like a complete product brand while retaining the otter root.

### Secondary directions

- **looplane** if water-world mood matters more than literal otter recognition.
- **Rillumi** if the brand should feel calm, bright, and broadly extensible.
- **Covami** if “sheltered companion” is a stronger positioning idea than animal specificity.

## Eliminated during lightweight search

- **Lutriva**: already used as an otter-derived fictional creature in PokéFarm Q and also appears on consumer goods listings.
- **Lutella**: existing Japanese beauty-equipment/product name.
- **Lutava**: active wellness brand and a registered US word mark in another class.
- **Lutrena**: established ILO program acronym/name.
- **Lutrana**: historical commercial scale company/brand.
- **Ottera**: high search noise and exact GitHub projects.
- **Ottara**: active company and a current US trademark application.
- **Ottura**: active digital/education business covering coding and ChatGPT-related offerings.
- **Ottilo**: active websites/automation business.
- **Ottori**: active programming/AI-related company.
- **Lutori**: an active programming-related company surfaced, too close to the category.
- **Lutide**: visually collides with the drug-name stem in semaglutide-related material.

## Collision-check ledger

### What was checked

- Exact distribution-name requests to the official PyPI JSON endpoint.
- Exact package-name requests to the official npm registry endpoint.
- Exact and `in:name` repository searches through GitHub's API until its rate limit was reached.
- Quoted general web searches for the leading names and suspicious candidates.
- Direct review of the ITIS *Lutra* taxonomy page.

All 28 longlist names returned HTTP 404 from PyPI and npm at the time of checking. A 404 only means an exact package record was not returned at that moment; it does **not** reserve the name or establish legal availability.

### Coverage limitations

- GitHub coverage is incomplete because the API rate limit was reached mid-list. Lutrali and Lutrilo were checked before the limit; the remaining names rely on quoted web search plus registries.
- General web-search findings are discovery-level. Only the ITIS taxonomy page was opened and read directly for the underlying otter-root claim.
- No comprehensive WIPO, USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO, J-PlatPat, or TIPO search was performed for the shortlist.
- No company-register, app-store, domain-name, social-handle, phonetic-similarity, or common-law use clearance was completed.

## Recommended next gate

Do not rename the project yet. Advance **Lutrali, Lutrilo, Lutruna, Lutrimi, and Lutrimo** to the formal screening phase:

1. Full software/project/package search, including GitHub after rate reset, app stores, Homebrew, crates.io, RubyGems, domains, and social handles.
2. Exact and phonetic trademark search in WIPO, USPTO, EUIPO, and TIPO, prioritizing Nice classes 9 and 42 and adjacent class 38.
3. Native-speaker pronunciation/spelling test with at least five English speakers: hear the name once, then spell it.
4. Otter-recognition test with and without the mascot. If the name alone cannot evoke an otter, verify that the combined name-plus-mark still produces strong recall.
5. Only after those gates, choose the brand/display name, distribution name, and CLI command together.

Current brand recommendation: **Lutrali** has the strongest strategy; **Lutrilo** is the safer verbal/usability challenger.
