# Preliminary trademark screen: Lutrimi, Lutrilo, Lutruna, Otrilo

Date checked: 2026-08-22  
Scope: exact marks and selected prefix/one-edit variants, emphasizing software, telecommunications, and technology services in Nice classes 9, 38, and 42.  
Nature of work: preliminary naming screen only; not legal advice or a freedom-to-operate opinion.

## Recommendation

Rank the candidates:

1. **Lutrimi** — best survivor
2. **Lutrilo** — also survives the verified US screen
3. **Lutruna** — usable only after manual international/EU follow-up; a dead historical WIPO mark, LUTRANA, is a near spelling
4. **Otrilo** — reject; an active exact-field near mark, OTIRILO, is only one inserted letter away in US class 9, and OTRIUM is active in classes 9 and 42

The defensible current conclusion is narrower than “globally available”: **Lutrimi and Lutrilo have no material live hit in the official USPTO class 9/38/42 searches performed.** WIPO and EUIPO did not provide a complete reliable absence check for those two names, so a manual attorney/database search is still required before adoption or filing.

## Candidate results

### 1. Lutrimi — best preliminary survivor

USPTO official live-record searches:

- Exact `LUTRIMI`, classes 9/38/42: **0 results**.
- Prefix `LUTRI*`, classes 9/38/42: **0 results**.
- One-edit fuzzy `LUTRIMI~1`, classes 9/38/42: **0 results**.

WIPO / EU:

- Madrid Monitor exact and narrow-prefix requests returned the portal error `invalid input - please reload the page`; this is not evidence of absence.
- No official indexed WIPO result surfaced in secondary web discovery, but search-engine non-discovery is not a clearance result.
- EUIPO/TMview automated exact-result retrieval was blocked/unreliable. No complete EU claim is made.

Assessment: **best candidate of the four**, because it is more distinctive and the verified US software/technology screen is clean. It still needs manual WIPO, EUIPO/TMview, target-country, and common-law checking.

### 2. Lutrilo — second preliminary survivor

USPTO official live-record searches:

- Exact `LUTRILO`, classes 9/38/42: **0 results**.
- Prefix `LUTRI*`, classes 9/38/42: **0 results**.
- One-edit fuzzy `LUTRILO~1`, classes 9/38/42: **0 results**.

WIPO / EU:

- Madrid Monitor exact and narrow-prefix requests returned the same portal input error; no verified absence claim is made.
- EUIPO/TMview could not be comprehensively queried through the automated route.

Assessment: **also survives the verified US screen**. It ranks behind Lutrimi because its shorter, Romance-style ending is less distinctive as a brand construction, not because a material US trademark collision was found.

### 3. Lutruna — survivor with a historical near mark

USPTO official live-record searches:

- Exact `LUTRUNA`, classes 9/38/42: **0 results**.
- Prefix `LUTRU*`, classes 9/38/42: **0 results**.
- One-edit fuzzy `LUTRUNA~1`, classes 9/38/42: **0 results**.

WIPO Madrid Monitor returned:

| Mark | IR | Holder | Status | Classes | Relevance |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| LUTRANA | 156379 | NS LUTRANA SAS | Deleted/non-renewed | 7, 9 | One-vowel spelling variant in class 9, but not live |

Assessment: the dead historical record is not an automatic blocker, but it shows the sound/spelling space is not wholly novel. Rank below Lutrimi/Lutrilo pending ownership/history and EU checks.

### 4. Otrilo — reject

USPTO exact `OTRILO`, classes 9/38/42: **0 results**, but the near-mark screen found material live records:

| Mark | US record | Owner | Status | Classes | Relevant goods/services |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| OTIRILO | Serial 88343397; Reg. 6050814 | Ningbo Puguang Lighting Technology Co., Ltd. (last listed owner) | Live registered, 2020-05-12 | 9 | Data cables |
| OTRIUM | Serial 79320033; Reg. 6897589 | Otrium Holding B.V. | Live registered, 2022-11-15 | 9, 25, 35, 42 | Downloadable/recorded retail software; hosting and programming e-commerce platforms |
| OTRIUM | Serial 97216473; Reg. 7255895 | Otrium Holding B.V. | Live registered, 2023-12-26 | 9, 35, 42 | Mobile/retail software and SaaS for an e-commerce platform |
| OWRILO | Serial 88544296; Reg. 6190903 | Li, Yafeng | Live registered, 2020-11-03 | 9 | Smartphone accessories and wearable electronics |

The strongest problem is **OTIRILO**, which differs from OTRILO by a single inserted `I` and is an active class 9 registration. OTRIUM is less similar as a complete word but already occupies the `OTRI-` software/SaaS space.

WIPO Madrid Monitor independently verifies **OTRIUM**, International Registration **1612502**, owned by Otrium Holding B.V., active in classes 9, 25, 35, and 42. The official WIPO query returned **0 exact results for OTRILO**, but that does not neutralize the active US near mark OTIRILO.

Assessment: **reject Otrilo**. Its naming advantage is not worth the avoidable class 9 similarity risk.

## Before choosing the final name

1. Manually run exact, phonetic, begins-with, and fuzzy searches for **Lutrimi** and **Lutrilo** in WIPO Global Brand Database, Madrid Monitor, EUIPO eSearch/TMview, and the Taiwan TIPO database.
2. Search company names, domains, GitHub, package registries, app stores, and actual product use; registered marks are only one part of naming risk.
3. Have counsel compare the intended class 9 downloadable software and class 42 SaaS/development-service descriptions against the closest results.
4. Re-check registry status immediately before public launch or filing.
