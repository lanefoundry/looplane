# Short euphonic otter-brand research

Date: 2026-08-22  
Brief: 4-6 ASCII letters, 2-3 syllables, spoken appeal first, warm but adult, suitable for a Python package/project and otter mascot.  
Status: preliminary naming research, not legal clearance; no repository rename performed.

## Conclusion

### New recommended short name: looplane

- Length: **6 letters**, one letter shorter than Lutrilo.
- Pronunciation: **rih-VOO-mee** (`ri-VU-mi`).
- Sound: the clean consonant/vowel alternation makes it smoother in speech than Lutrilo. The middle stress gives it a memorable rise and the final `-mi` lands softly.
- Story: a coinage combining a `riv-` river cue with Japanese `umi` (sea). It creates a water-world feeling, while the mascot supplies the actual otter recognition.
- Tone: warmer and cuter than Lutrilo, but still usable as a project name if the visual identity stays restrained.
- Technical namespace screen: no exact PyPI, npm, GitHub user/org, or exact repository collision was found at the time of checking; general product discovery found no exact software/product/company collision.
- Taiwan preliminary trademark screen: no exact/full-prefix mark found in classes 9, 38, or 42. `RIVIAN` is a nearby strong `RIV-` family and warrants caution, but differs materially in the fourth letter, ending, and spoken form.
- US preliminary trademark screen: official live USPTO exact, narrow-prefix, and one-edit searches in classes 9, 38, and 42 returned zero. A broader `RIVU*` search had five more-distant live results, with no decisive blocker found.
- WIPO: exact search returned zero, but prefix/fuzzy requests failed at the official portal. EUIPO/TMview was unavailable, so no worldwide-clearance claim is made.

Suggested first-use lockup:

> **looplane** *(rih-VOO-mee)* — a little otter who stays afloat while you build.

## Why there is no clean 4-5 letter recommendation

Two independent ideation passes produced 43 unique 4-5 letter candidates. Every one had at least one material collision: an exact package, GitHub namespace/repository, or an active exact-name software/app/product.

Representative eliminations:

- `Luro`: active design-system platform, AI products, and apps.
- `Luvo`: active personal AI companion and multiple apps.
- `Lurio`: active AI presentation product and expense-tracking app.
- `Lumo`: Proton's direct-category AI assistant plus occupied packages.
- `Elori`: active AI sales-agent product.
- `Eluvo`: active camera app, software business, and vision-AI product.
- `Oruvi`: existing public-sector app/project.
- `Arovi`, `Miora`, `Orivo`, `Aluma`, and `Evaro`: active AI/software products in adjacent categories.

The evidence supports a practical boundary: **six letters is currently the shortest length that produced a clean preliminary survivor without using an awkward spelling.** A four- or five-letter choice would require accepting a known collision, buying an existing namespace, or using a two-word brand.
