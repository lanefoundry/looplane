# Preliminary trademark screen: LUTUNO

Date checked: 2026-08-22  
Scope: exact, prefix, and one-edit screening, emphasizing software, telecommunications, and technology services in Nice classes 9, 38, and 42.  
Nature of work: preliminary naming screen only; not legal advice or a freedom-to-operate opinion.

## Decision

**PASS — preliminary screen.**

`LUTUNO` passes the official USPTO screen performed for live records in classes 9, 38, and 42. WIPO Madrid Monitor also returned zero exact and zero `LUTUN*` prefix records. It is therefore a stronger preliminary survivor than a candidate with a material live software mark.

This does **not** mean globally available or legally cleared. EUIPO/TMview was not reliably searchable through the automated route, WIPO's fuzzy query failed, and no common-law/company-name/use investigation was performed.
